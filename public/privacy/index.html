Privacy Policy

Data Handling, On-Device Architecture & Third-Party Integrations

Vibra Music is built as a local-first, open-source Android audio client licensed under GNU GPLv3. We operate no user account database, deploy no advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs, and perform neural DSP audio analysis strictly on your device. Any external transmission occurs only when you stream media, enable optional integrations, or join a Listen Together session.

01.

Scope & Local-First Data Posture

This Privacy Policy governs the handling of information in connection with the Vibra Music Android application (application identifier com.fortune.vibramusic), the optional Listen Together synchronization relay, and this official Vibra Music website (collectively, the "Software").

Vibra Music is maintained as free, open-source software by independent contributors. Unlike commercial streaming platforms, Vibra Music maintains no centralized user registry, operates no social graph, and does not harvest, aggregate, sell, or broker personal data. Subject to the opt-in third-party services detailed below, your library, playback history, acoustic mix profiles, and preferences remain resident on your device.

02.

Information Stored & Processed Strictly On-Device

Core playback, organization, and DSP features execute locally without transmitting records to Vibra Music maintainers:

  • Playback History, Playlists & Statistics: Listening records, play counts, custom playlists, cached artwork, and user-supplied lyrics (CustomLyricsStore) are stored in local application databases on your Android device.
  • On-Device Neural DSP & Mix Analysis: Beat tracking, vocal stem isolation, harmonic Camelot key detection, and crossfade planning run locally via ONNX Runtime models (beat_this_int8.onnx and vocals_umxhq_int8.onnx) and native JNI audio libraries. Raw audio files and acoustic fingerprints are never uploaded to external Vibra servers.
  • Hardware-Backed Credential Storage: Authentication tokens and session cookies you optionally provide (such as YouTube Music, Spotify sp_dc, Discord, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Google Drive, or WebDAV credentials) are persisted inside EncryptedSharedPreferences wrapped by Google Tink keysets bound to the Android Keystore, which never leaves the device. On certain OEM Android builds where hardware Keystore verification is unavailable, the application degrades to private app-sandboxed preferences rather than failing launch.
03.

Ephemeral Data in "Listen Together" Sessions

If you voluntarily create or join a Listen Together room using a six-character invite code, your device opens a TLS-encrypted WebSocket connection to the configured party relay (by default https://party.vibramusic.store, or any self-hosted server address you enter in settings).

The Listen Together Go relay server operates strictly in volatile memory with no backing database. It processes only transient synchronization payloads necessary to keep up to five connected devices aligned: track identifiers, playhead position (positionMs), monotonic server clock anchors (anchorMs), play/pause state, and NTP-style round-trip clock pings. Room state is discarded immediately upon session termination and is never persisted or mined.

04.

Direct Third-Party Services, Google API Limited Use & Optional Integrations

Vibra Music's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

When you stream content or explicitly enable external connectors, your device communicates directly with the respective third-party provider over HTTPS (cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled at the manifest level via usesCleartextTraffic="false"). Vibra Music does not proxy or log your credentials through intermediary developer servers. Each external provider acts as an independent data controller governed by its own privacy terms:

  • Google APIs & YouTube Data Limited Use Compliance: When you choose to sign in with your Google or YouTube account, credentials and OAuth tokens are stored exclusively inside hardware-wrapped Android Keystore EncryptedSharedPreferences on your local device. We access your account strictly to retrieve your playlists, liked tracks, and listening suggestions. We do NOT transfer, sell, disclose, or broker your Google user data to any third party, data broker, advertising network, or analytics vendor. Google user data is NEVER used for serving advertisements or promotional profiling. You can disconnect your Google account at any time in app settings or revoke access via Google Account Permissions (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • YouTube Music & Audio Playback: Search queries, audio stream requests, PoToken session generation, and playback interactions communicate directly between your device and official YouTube / Google InnerTube endpoints.
  • Google Drive Backup (Opt-In): If enabled under Settings → Backup and Restore, Vibra Music writes encrypted structured backups of your listening statistics and preferences directly into your private Google Drive application folder (drive.appdata scope) on your chosen cadence (Off, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly). Maintainers have zero visibility into your Google Drive files, and no Google Drive files are accessible to third parties.
  • Discord Rich Presence (Opt-In): When authenticated in-app, your client transmits your active track title, artist, album metadata, and elapsed playback timestamps directly to Discord gateways.
  • Last.fm & ListenBrainz Scrobbling (Opt-In): When configured, track playback completions ("scrobbles") and "now playing" status updates are sent directly to Last.fm or ListenBrainz using your session credentials.
  • Spotify Web Session & Canvas (Opt-In): If you supply a Spotify session cookie (sp_dc) to retrieve animated album canvases or import playlists, requests go directly from your device to Spotify endpoints. You may revoke access at any time via Spotify's "Sign out everywhere" control or by clearing local tokens in Vibra Music settings.
  • Pluggable Module Sources, Lyrics Providers & WebDAV: Vibra Music supports user-installed JavaScript module sources (executed via QuickJS), external word-synced lyrics sources (such as Musixmatch, LRCLIB, KuGou, and Genius), and personal WebDAV servers. Any network requests initiated by a module or custom server you configure are directed solely to those configured endpoints.
05.

Android System Permissions & Purpose Limitation

Vibra Music declares only permissions directly required for audio playback, local library management, and hardware interoperability:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream media, resolve artwork/lyrics, and enforce your separate Wi-Fi versus mobile data audio quality ceilings.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE (Media Playback & Data Sync), WAKE_LOCK & POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Maintains uninterrupted background playback, Android Auto / Wear OS / Samsung Now Bar media controls, and reliable offline downloads after you leave the screen.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (and Legacy Storage on Android ≤ 12): Reads your local device audio library and saves offline tracks with embedded metadata to the shared Music directory via Android MediaStore.
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT (Android 12+): Requested only when you first open the audio output picker so paired headsets display their actual device names instead of generic system labels. Declining it does not impair playback.
  • RECORD_AUDIO & Speech Recognition: Invoked solely upon explicit user action for voice search queries or audio-reactive features. Microphone input is never captured or transmitted in the background.
  • VIBRATE: Provides subtle tactile feedback while respecting your operating system's global haptics toggle.
06.

Data Retention, Erasure & Your Rights

Because Vibra Music stores no centralized copies of your personal data, you exercise direct, immediate control over retention and erasure. At any time, you may sign out of individual integrations, purge cached media, delete local listening history, or uninstall the application to permanently erase all local databases and Android Keystore-wrapped credentials.

Note regarding Android System Backup: The application manifest permits standard Android backup rules (backup_rules.xml / data_extraction_rules.xml). Hardware-wrapped Tink keysets cannot be unwrapped after transfer to a new device; you may disable OS-level backup for Vibra Music inside your Android device settings at any time.

Depending on your jurisdiction (including residents of the EEA, UK, California, or Nigeria under the NDPA), you hold statutory rights of access, correction, portability, and deletion. For data held by third-party services you connected (such as Google, Discord, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, or Spotify), please exercise those rights directly through the respective provider's privacy portal.

07.

Children's Privacy, Amendments & Contact

Vibra Music is a general-purpose audio client not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

We may revise this Privacy Policy as the codebase evolves—for instance, when new opt-in integrations or permissions are introduced. Material revisions will be reflected in the effective date above and committed transparently to the public repository history.

Questions, security disclosures, or privacy inquiries may be submitted via our public GitHub repository (Fortunehack45/Vibra_Music), our dedicated Security Policy page, or the official Telegram community.
