Privacy Policy

Data Handling, On-Device Architecture & Google API Services Disclosure

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 Version: Rev. 2.1 Package ID: com.fortune.vibramusic Publisher: Fortunehack45
Core Summary: Vibra Music is built as a local-first, open-source Android audio client licensed under GNU GPLv3. We operate no user account database, deploy no advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs, and perform neural DSP audio analysis strictly on your device. Any external network activity occurs only when you search/stream media from upstream platforms, enable optional third-party integrations, or join a Listen Together room.

01. Scope & Local-First Architecture

This Privacy Policy governs the handling of information in connection with the Vibra Music Android application (application identifier com.fortune.vibramusic), the optional Listen Together synchronization relay, and the official website at https://vibramusic.store/ (collectively, the "Software").

Vibra Music is maintained as free, open-source software. Unlike commercial streaming platforms, Vibra Music maintains no centralized user registry, operates no behavioral profiling, and does not harvest, aggregate, sell, or broker personal data. All playback history, playlists, cached lyrics, and user preferences remain resident on your local Android device.

02. Information Stored & Processed Strictly On-Device

Core music playback, organization, and DSP features execute locally without transmitting records to Vibra Music maintainers:

  • Playback History & Playlists: Listening logs, play counts, custom playlists, and user-supplied lyrics are saved in local SQLite/Room databases on your Android device.
  • On-Device Neural DSP & Mix Analysis: Beat tracking, vocal stem separation, and harmonic Camelot key detection run locally via quantized ONNX Runtime models (beat_this_int8.onnx and vocals_umxhq_int8.onnx) and native JNI C++ code. Raw audio and acoustic fingerprints are never uploaded to any external server.
  • Hardware-Backed Credential Storage: Authentication tokens and session cookies you optionally provide are persisted inside EncryptedSharedPreferences wrapped by Google Tink keysets bound to the Android Keystore, which never leaves your device.

03. Google User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:

Vibra Music's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

When you choose to connect your Google / YouTube account in Vibra Music:

  • Purpose of Access: We access your account strictly upon your direct request to retrieve your personal playlists, liked songs, and music recommendations via official YouTube / Google InnerTube APIs.
  • No Third-Party Transfer: We do NOT transfer, sell, disclose, or broker your Google user data to any third party, data broker, advertising network, or analytics vendor.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is NEVER used for serving advertisements, retargeting, or promotional profiling.
  • No Human Inspection: Maintainers and developers have zero access to your Google account credentials or user data. No human reads or reviews your Google data.
  • Direct Connection: All API communications occur directly between your device and Google servers over HTTPS with strict TLS encryption. No credentials pass through any intermediary developer server.

04. Ephemeral Data in "Listen Together" Sessions

If you voluntarily create or join a Listen Together room using a six-character room code, your device connects to the configured relay (by default party.vibramusic.store over TLS WebSocket).

The Listen Together relay server runs entirely in volatile RAM with no database. It processes only transient synchronization payloads: track IDs, millisecond playhead anchors, playback state, and clock pings to keep up to five connected devices aligned. Room state is discarded immediately upon room closure and is never stored.

05. Android System Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream music, fetch album artwork, and enforce separate Wi-Fi vs mobile streaming quality limits.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE (Media Playback & Data Sync): Maintains uninterrupted playback and system media controls (Android Auto, Wear OS, lockscreen controls) when the screen is off.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / Storage: Accesses your local audio files to play your on-device music library.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays active playback notification controls on Android 13+.

06. Data Retention, User Rights & Data Deletion

Because Vibra Music stores no centralized user data on external servers, you have complete control over data deletion:

  • Immediate Deletion: You can wipe all cached data, search history, and saved credentials at any time in the app via Settings → Clear Cache or by clearing app data in Android System Settings.
  • Account Disconnection: You can disconnect your Google account at any time in Settings → Account, which immediately deletes local session tokens. You may also revoke access at any time via Google Account Permissions.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the application permanently removes all local databases and encrypted keys from your device.
