Third-Party Open-Source Acknowledgements

Vibra Music stands on the work of the open-source audio and mobile ecosystem. Key upstream attributions preserved in the repository include:

  • Apple-Style Lyrics Animation: Word- and syllable-synced lyrics motion architecture credited to binimum (github.com/binimum/am-lyrics).
